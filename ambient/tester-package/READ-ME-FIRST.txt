ACRE2 ambient battle sound — test build
=======================================

What this does
--------------
A modified ACRE2 TeamSpeak plugin. When you transmit on an ACRE2 radio, it
captures your own Arma 3 audio and mixes it into what you're sending, so
listeners hear the gunfire and explosions around you riding under your voice.

It only captures Arma 3's own process, never your speakers or your microphone
mix, so nothing else you have playing can leak into the radio.

This is a private test build. It is not official ACRE2 and not signed by the
ACRE2 team. Source: https://github.com/ljackson330/acre2 (branch
ambient-battle-sound). It's GPLv3, same as ACRE2 itself.


Before you start
----------------
- Your ACRE2 must be version 2.14.0.1064. Check in-game or in the Workshop
  item; if yours is newer, stop and tell me, the plugin won't match.
- You need TeamSpeak 3 (not TeamSpeak 5/6) and the 64-bit client.
- Back up your existing plugin first — step 2 tells you where.


Step 1 — pre-flight, 30 seconds
-------------------------------
Start Arma 3 and get to the main menu, so it's making some noise.

Then run ambient-probe.exe from a terminal. It records 20 seconds of Arma's
audio and tells you what it found:

    ambient-probe.exe

You want to see:

    OK: activated. negotiated format: 48000 Hz, 1 ch, 16-bit
    ...
    peak : -XX.X dBFS

If it says FAIL, stop here and send me the output. That's the whole point of
this step — it tells us whether capture works at all without you having to
install anything or get in-game.

It writes ambient-probe.wav next to itself. Send me that too.


Step 2 — install the plugin
---------------------------
IMPORTANT: it has to go in BOTH places, or Arma will silently put the old one
back next time you launch.

  a) Your TeamSpeak plugins folder:
       %APPDATA%\TS3Client\plugins\
     Rename the existing acre2_win64.dll to acre2_win64.dll.backup first.
     Copy acre2_win64.dll from this folder in.

  b) Your @acre2 mod folder, in the "plugin" subfolder:
       ...\steamapps\workshop\content\107410\<id>\plugin\
     (or wherever you have @acre2). Same thing: back up the existing
     acre2_win64.dll, copy this one in.

If you only do (a), ACRE2 copies the mod folder's copy over it on the next Arma
launch and you'll be testing stock ACRE2 without realising.

Restart TeamSpeak completely after copying.


Step 3 — confirm it's live
--------------------------
Find your acre2.ini:

    %APPDATA%\TS3Client\acre\acre2.ini

Add this line at the end:

    ambientSelfTest = true;

Restart TeamSpeak. Start Arma and let it make noise. Then check:

    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Arma 3\acre2_plugin.log

You want a line like:

    AMBIENT SELFTEST: OK -- backend 'WASAPI process loopback', 383040 samples...

If you see "FAILED", send me that log. Once you've seen OK, you can remove the
ambientSelfTest line (it just adds an 8 second capture at startup).


Important: this build is not tuned
----------------------------------
There is a DSP chain -- a microphone model and a compressor -- that tames the
gunfire and keeps quieter background audio audible. It currently only runs on
the Linux side of this project, so THIS build does not have it.

What that means for you: gunfire will be noticeably louder and more dynamic
than the version I have been listening to, and busy scenes may clip. That is
known, and not what I need feedback on. What I need to know is whether the
capture works at all on real Windows with real Arma.


Step 4 — the actual test
------------------------
Get in game with me. Find some combat, key up, and talk normally. I'll tell you
how it sounds from the other end.

Things worth reporting:
- Is my voice still clear, or is the ambience burying it?
- Does the combat audio sound like combat, or like mush?
- Anything weird when you start/stop transmitting?


To undo all of this
-------------------
Put your backed-up acre2_win64.dll back in both folders and restart TeamSpeak.
Remove the ambientSelfTest line from acre2.ini. Nothing else was changed.


Known things
------------
- If TeamSpeak says "Failed to load plugin", you're missing the DirectX
  runtime. Unlikely if ACRE2 already works for you, since stock ACRE2 needs it
  too. Installing the DirectX End-User Runtime fixes it.
- The very first transmission after starting TeamSpeak may have no ambience.
  That's deliberate — it's how the plugin works out which capture method to
  use without delaying your push-to-talk. From the second key-up on it's normal.
